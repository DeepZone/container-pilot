# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, focus on technical merit, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publication of another person's private information.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- showing empathy and patience
- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes
- respecting different levels of experience and different perspectives

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, abusive, or harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing them.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
